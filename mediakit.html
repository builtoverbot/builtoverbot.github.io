About Bob & Sandy

We are Built Over Bot—a husband-and-wife design and maker team based in rural Illinois. On our YouTube channel and platforms, we document our journey from 3D printing custom mechanical art, fidget creations, and decorative electronics to our massive undertaking: transforming a local local hardware store into MakerStudio, a community creative makerspace. Partnering with us offers direct exposure to an enthusiastic community of makers, designers, engineers, and hobbyists.
